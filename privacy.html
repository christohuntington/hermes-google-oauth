PERSONAL GOOGLE WORKSPACE CONNECTION

Privacy policy

This policy covers the independently operated personal Google OAuth integration identified as Hermes Agent. It is not a privacy policy for Google, Nous Research, or every installation of Hermes Agent.

Google account access

Depending on the permissions granted, the integration can read or change Gmail messages, calendar information, contacts, files, documents, spreadsheets, and tasks. Google displays the requested permissions during authorization. The operator controls the connected account and the workflows that use it.

Use and processing

Google data is used for operator-directed personal assistance and authorized automations. Relevant content may be processed in the operator's environment and by the AI or integration providers configured for the workflow. This is not a promise that all processing stays on the local device.

The permitted use of Google data excludes sale, targeted advertising, and training general-purpose AI models. Connected providers must be configured and used consistently with these restrictions. Use and transfer of Google API information must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and disclosure

The operator controls retained OAuth credentials, workflow records, and local files. Task-relevant content may also be retained by configured providers under their own policies and the operator's settings. This public static site contains no Google account records, OAuth credentials, or account-access functionality.

Revocation and deletion

The operator can revoke access through Google account connections, stop the relevant automations, and delete retained local records. Revoking access prevents future authorized access but does not itself erase previously retained records. Copies held by connected providers are subject to their deletion controls.

Contact

Questions and deletion requests should go to the operator through the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
